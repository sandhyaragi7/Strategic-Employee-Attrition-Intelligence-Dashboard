Strategic Employee Attrition Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Strategic Employee Attrition Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and identify important workforce trends.

The dashboard provides insights into employee turnover, employee demographics, job-related factors, overtime, salary hikes, years at the company, and burnout levels. It helps HR teams understand patterns related to employee attrition and supports data-driven workforce analysis.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Compare employees who are still working with employees who have left.
- Identify factors associated with employee attrition.
- Analyze overtime and burnout levels.
- Understand the relationship between salary hikes and employee retention.
- Analyze employee experience using years at the company.
- Present HR data through interactive Power BI visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

🔄 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared using Power Query. The data cleaning process included:

- Removing duplicate records
- Handling data inconsistencies
- Transforming columns
- Creating required calculated columns
- Creating bands for overtime hours, burnout score, and salary hike percentage

🧮 DAX

DAX measures and calculated columns were created to calculate important HR metrics and categorize employee information into meaningful groups.

Examples include:

- Employee Attrition
- Employees Left
- Employees Still Working
- Average Years at Company
- Overtime Hours Band
- Burnout Score Band
- Salary Hike Band
